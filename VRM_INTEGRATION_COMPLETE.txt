🎨 HOW IT WORKS:

Flow:
  User Speaks
     ↓
  Gemini Processes
     ↓
  Audio Output Starts → _play_audio() triggered
     ↓
  start_speaking() called → VRM Lip Sync ON
     ↓
  Text Transcription → detect_mood_from_text()
     ↓
  set_expression_from_text() → VRM Expression Updates
     ↓
  Audio Finishes
     ↓
  stop_speaking() called → VRM Lip Sync OFF
